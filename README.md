# Flappy Sprig

![Flappy Sprig](https://i.ibb.co/YOUR-IMAGE-ID/Screenshot-2026-09-29-234421.png)


### A simple Flappy Bird-style arcade game built for the Hack Club Sprig engine. Fly through the gaps, avoid the pipes, and try to beat your highest score.

#### The Game

* Flappy Sprig: Control the bird and fly through the gaps between the pipes.
* Pipes: New pipes keep appearing from the right side and move across the screen.
* Score: Get 1 point every time you successfully pass a pipe.
* Difficulty: The game gets faster as your score increases.
* High Score: Your best score is kept while you play.
* Sounds: Includes sounds for flapping, scoring, starting, and hitting an obstacle.
* Pause: Pause and continue the game whenever you want.
* Restart: Start a new game after getting game over.

#### Mechanics

* Gravity: The bird slowly falls down unless you flap.
* Random Gaps: Each new pipe gets a random gap position.
* Increasing Speed: Every 5 points, the game gets a little faster.
* Collision: Hitting a pipe or the ground ends the game.
* Infinite Pipes: Pipes keep spawning while you play and are removed after leaving the screen.

#### Controls

* W / I: Make the bird fly up
* J: Pause / continue
* K: Start / restart the game

Controls are also shown on the start screen and during the game.

#### About

I made this as a simple arcade game for Sprig and built it up step by step. I started with the basic bird movement and then added pipes, scoring, collision, sounds, pausing, and increasing difficulty.

The game is intentionally pretty simple, but I wanted to make it feel more complete than just a basic Flappy Bird clone.

No gambling or anything like that here — just try to beat your high score :)
